# FrameForge AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 31, 2026

FrameForge AI is a screenshot design studio for creating app screenshots, social graphics, mockups, launch visuals, and marketing assets.

## Data Collection

FrameForge AI does not require an account and does not collect personal data for developer analytics, advertising, or tracking in the current version.

## Photos and Screenshots

If you choose to import screenshots or images, they are used inside the app so you can create designs and exports. Imported screenshots are stored locally on your device unless you choose to share or export them.

## Voice Input

FrameForge AI includes optional voice input for dictating design prompts and caption ideas. Voice recognition uses Apple's system speech recognition services. Voice input is only used when you start recording.

## Purchases

Subscription scaffolding uses Apple's StoreKit framework. Purchase and subscription processing is handled by Apple.

## Third-Party Services

The current version uses mock AI by default and does not require a remote AI account. If future versions connect to an online AI service, this policy will be updated before release.
